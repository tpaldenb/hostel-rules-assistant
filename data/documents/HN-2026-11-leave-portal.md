## HN-2026-11.1 | Overnight leave application
Overnight leave must now be applied for on the Campus ERP portal under Hostel > Leave, at least 48 hours before departure. The block warden approves the request on the portal. Paper leave forms are no longer accepted.
supersedes: HM-3.2

## HN-2026-11.2 | Long leave of more than three nights
For leave of more than three nights, apply on the Campus ERP portal and upload a signed consent letter from a parent or guardian. The application must be made at least 48 hours before departure.
supersedes: HM-3.3
