## TM-1.1 | Route 1 - City Centre
Route 1 runs Main Gate, Market Square, Town Hall, City Centre on weekdays. Buses leave the Main Gate at 8:00 AM, 1:00 PM and 5:30 PM. The last bus back leaves City Centre at 6:45 PM.

## TM-1.2 | Route 2 - Railway Station
Route 2 runs Main Gate, Lake Road, Old Bus Stand, Central Railway Station on weekdays. Buses leave the Main Gate at 7:00 AM, 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM. The last bus back leaves the station at 7:30 PM.

## TM-1.3 | Route 3 - Hillside Colony
Route 3 runs Hillside Colony, Sector 4, Main Gate on weekdays. The morning bus leaves Hillside Colony at 7:40 AM. The evening bus leaves the Main Gate at 5:15 PM.

## TM-1.4 | Route 4 - Airport Road
Route 4 runs Main Gate, IT Park, Airport Road Junction on Saturdays only. Buses leave the Main Gate at 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

## TM-2.1 | Sunday shuttle
A shuttle runs between the Main Gate and City Centre every hour from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Sundays.

## TM-3.1 | Semester bus pass
A semester bus pass costs Rs 2,400 and covers all routes. Apply at the Transport Office with your student ID card.

## TM-3.2 | Lost bus pass
A lost bus pass must be reported to the Transport Office. A replacement pass costs Rs 150.

## TM-3.3 | Travelling without a pass
Students without a valid bus pass must buy a single-trip ticket for Rs 30 from the conductor.

## TM-3.4 | Service on holidays
Buses do not run on national holidays. Service on other institute holidays is announced by notice.
