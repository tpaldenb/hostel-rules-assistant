## TN-2026-03.1 | Route 2 - Railway Station
Route 2 now runs Main Gate, Lake Road, New Bus Terminal, Central Railway Station on weekdays. The Old Bus Stand stop is discontinued. Buses leave the Main Gate at 7:00 AM, 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM. The last bus back leaves the station at 7:30 PM.
