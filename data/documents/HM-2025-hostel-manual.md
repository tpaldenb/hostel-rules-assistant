## HM-2.1 | Weekday gate closing time
On weekdays (Monday to Friday) the hostel main gate closes at 9:30 PM. All residents must be inside and sign the in-time register by then.

## HM-2.2 | Weekend and holiday gate closing time
On Saturdays, Sundays and institute holidays the hostel main gate closes at 10:00 PM.

## HM-2.3 | Morning gate opening
The hostel main gate opens at 5:30 AM every day. Residents may not leave the hostel before the gate opens except on approved emergency leave.

## HM-2.4 | Late entry procedure
A resident who arrives after the gate has closed must report to the security desk and record the reason in the late-entry register. The duty warden reviews the register the next morning. Three late entries in one calendar month are reported to the resident's parents or guardian.

## HM-2.5 | Late entry fine
Each late entry after the first in a calendar month carries a fine of Rs 100, added to the hostel dues.

## HM-2.6 | Library late pass
Residents holding a Library Late Pass may return to the hostel up to 10:30 PM on weekdays when returning from the central library. The pass is issued by the block warden and must be shown at the gate.

## HM-3.1 | Day outing
Residents may go out during the day without prior permission by signing the out-register at the gate. They must return before the gate closing time.

## HM-3.2 | Overnight leave application
A resident who will spend a night away from the hostel must submit the paper leave form to the block warden at least 24 hours before departure.

## HM-3.3 | Long leave of more than three nights
Leave of more than three nights requires a signed consent letter from a parent or guardian, submitted with the leave form to the block warden.

## HM-3.4 | Medical leave
A resident who is unwell and needs to go home must get a certificate from the institute medical officer. The block warden must be informed within 24 hours of leaving.

## HM-3.5 | Emergency leave
In a family or medical emergency, call the duty warden on the hostel helpline for approval by phone. The leave record must be completed within two days of returning.

## HM-3.6 | Overstaying approved leave
A resident who returns after the approved leave has ended without informing the warden is fined Rs 200 for each extra day.

## HM-4.1 | Mess timings
Breakfast is served 7:15 AM to 9:00 AM, lunch 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM, evening snacks 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM and dinner 7:30 PM to 9:15 PM.

## HM-4.2 | Mess rebate during leave
Residents on approved leave of four or more consecutive days may claim a mess rebate. The claim must reach the mess office within seven days of returning.

## HM-5.1 | Visitors and meeting hours
Parents and guardians may meet residents in the Visitors' Lounge between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Visitors must sign in at the main gate and show photo ID.

## HM-5.2 | Overnight guests
Overnight guests are not permitted in hostel rooms. Visitors are not allowed on residential floors.

## HM-6.1 | Quiet hours
Quiet hours run from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM. Music and group activity in rooms and corridors are not allowed during this time.

## HM-6.2 | Room change requests
A room change may be requested once per semester, in the first two weeks of the semester, through the block warden.
