## HN-2026-19.1 | Weekday gate closing time
After review with the Student Council, the hostel main gate closes at 9:15 PM on weekdays (Monday to Friday) from 1 September 2026. All residents must be inside and sign the in-time register by then.
supersedes: HN-2026-04.1

## HN-2026-19.2 | Late entry fine
Each late entry after the first in a calendar month carries a fine of Rs 150, added to the hostel dues.
