## TN-2026-12.1 | Route 1 - City Centre
Because of flyover work, Route 1 will not stop at Market Square. Students should use the Town Hall stop instead. Route 1 runs Main Gate, Town Hall, City Centre on weekdays. Buses leave the Main Gate at 8:00 AM, 1:00 PM and 5:30 PM. The last bus back leaves City Centre at 6:45 PM. Allow an extra 10 minutes per trip.
